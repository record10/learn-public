About Record Learn

Learning Built for Kids & Curiosity

A gamified, offline-first learning platform for ages 3–16. Designed with science-backed mastery loops, zero tracking, and zero runtime dependencies.

100% Offline & Private

Zero telemetry, zero cookies, zero external trackers. All progress stays safely in your local browser storage.

Mastery-Based

Grounded in the Understand → Remember → Fluent cycle with misconception diagnosis, predictions, and spaced retrieval.

Instant & Lightweight

Static site generation with service worker caching. All icons and audio sprites are inlined and tree-shaken at build time.
